# Google Sites v1 migration — design brief

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** Proposed build sequence; Google Sites workspace is not connected.

## Goal
Rebuild the approved UP Economics Research Center mock as an editable native Google Site that faculty and staff can maintain with text, images, YouTube, and Google Drive. Visitor-facing copy is English.

## Operating model
Editors are not technical. Use native Sites sections and repeatable page patterns. A Center-owned Google Drive folder holds source content, approved media, and an editorial register. Sites pages are the public presentation; Drive is the editorial source of truth.

## Information architecture
Top navigation: Home · Research · People · Publications · Seminars · News · About. Research has one hub and nine nested area pages. People has Faculty Researchers and Research Professionals. Publications is one readable index grouped by type and year. Seminars puts confirmed upcoming events first, then General and Student series archives. News is dated and links to one canonical item. About holds scope, collaboration, and verified contact.

## Visual implementation
Use a built-in Google Sites theme; warm white/paper sections, charcoal text, UP burgundy/gold accents, strong type hierarchy, large photography, generous whitespace, and a restrained header/footer. Use native buttons, image layouts, collapsible groups, dividers, YouTube embeds, Drive embeds, and preview at phone/tablet/desktop sizes.

Google Sites cannot import the mock HTML/CSS as native editable pages. Its custom font file, live local search, JavaScript filters, hover states, scroll animation, and page transitions are not native features. Native Sites search and clear year/type sections are the v1 discovery pattern. YouTube and Drive content use native embeds. Embedded HTML is isolated from the page and is not the default basis for site navigation or catalogues.

## Data rules
Do not publish demo publications/news, unconfirmed dates, unverified affiliations, or unsupported contact details. Import only sourced team names, short biographies, and source-mapped portraits. Verify official area names and media reuse before launch.

## Page inventory
1 Home
2 Research (hub)
3–11 nine nested Research area pages
12 People
13 Publications
14 Seminars

16 About

## Editorial workflow
One shared Drive folder stores approved assets, the working register, and final PDFs. Give each item one canonical page or destination URL. Editors update the record once, update related links, and log verifier/date. A named Center owner controls Site and Drive ownership; a termly steward checks links, roster, publications, and event status.

## Acceptance conditions
- All 16 pages exist with correct hierarchy and working navigation.
- Twelve sourced profile entries connect to topic pages; portrait links work.
- Embedded video/files are viewable by intended visitors.
- Demo data is absent and citation links are checked.
- Site search, keyboard navigation, mobile/desktop previews, and viewer access are manually checked.
- Keep the Site a private draft until names, copy, media permissions, contact, ownership, intended public audience, and domain are approved.
