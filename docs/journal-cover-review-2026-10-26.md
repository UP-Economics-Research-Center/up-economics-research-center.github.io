## What editors need before replacing a photograph

1. Locate the article’s journal, volume, issue and publication date on its publisher page. Advance articles may not yet have an issue.
2. Find that issue’s official cover and confirm whether the license expressly permits this website use, including any separately credited artwork. An article’s open-access status alone does not settle cover rights.
3. If needed, obtain written permission from the publisher or the named cover rights holder. Record the scope, conditions, source and permission evidence. If permission is refused or unavailable, keep the existing licensed image and publisher link.
4. Save an authorized cover locally; retain the full cover without cropping. Add truthful EN/ES alternative text, credit, source URL and rights information. Review Research, the publication archive, the detail page and image credits before publication.
