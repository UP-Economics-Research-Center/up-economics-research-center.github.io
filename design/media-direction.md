# Media direction

## Home
Existing UP Internacional campus film, linked from UP’s institutional site, replaces the generated concept photograph. The original video is embedded on click with native player controls and a YouTube fallback link. No autoplay before user interaction. The film and its thumbnail are real existing assets. Embedded playback requires network access; playback in a local file may be restricted by YouTube.

## People
Twelve entries follow the source Center directory. All twelve portraits are now saved locally. Maria Jose Favela, Marytell, Juan Álvaro and Luciano were recovered using refreshed image URLs from the public source page. No faces or identities were generated. The public profile uses the full name Maria Jose Favela; Joshua M. Torres and Luciano A. Quintana retain documented abbreviations. ## Other pages
Keep Research and Publications typographic and easy to scan. Add figures to individual project pages when the figure belongs to that research. Use event photos, speaker portraits and recording thumbnails on actual seminar records. Use one documentary image for a featured news article when available. Do not repeat the large hero-video treatment on every page or fill missing research content with stock images. About remains text-led for now.

## Provenance and launch checks
