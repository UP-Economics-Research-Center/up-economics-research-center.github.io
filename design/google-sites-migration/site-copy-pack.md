### Education and Human Capital
Page path slug: education-and-human-capital
Studies how educational access, schooling delays, and learning quality shape opportunity and labor-market outcomes.

Documented team to verify: Akito Kamei; José Miguel Montoya Morales.

### Digital and Business Economics
Page path slug: digital-and-business-economics
Examines digital markets, financial technology, artificial intelligence, and how firms make decisions amid technological change.

Documented team to verify: Eugenio Gómez Alatorre; Esteban Colla de Robertis; Marytell Arciniega Castellanos.

### Political Economy
Page path slug: political-economy
Studies how political and economic institutions, incentives, and public policy shape development and social outcomes.

Documented team to verify: Esteban Colla de Robertis; Sergio Rodríguez García.

### Macroeconomics
Page path slug: macro-economy
Researches inflation, monetary policy, forecasting, and macroeconomic outcomes in Mexico.

Documented team to verify: Arnulfo Rodríguez Hernández; Esteban Colla de Robertis; Juan Álvaro Díaz Raimond Kedilhac.

### Development Economics
Page path slug: development-economics
Examines poverty, inequality, social mobility, and human development, often using causal empirical methods.

Documented team to verify: Akito Kamei; Marytell Arciniega Castellanos; Joshua M. Torres; Luciano A. Quintana.

### Health, Water and Sanitation
Page path slug: water-and-sanitation
Studies health, access to safe water and sanitation, and their effects on household well-being.

Documented team to verify: Akito Kamei; Juan Álvaro Díaz Raimond Kedilhac; Andrea Corte Aréchiga.

### Agricultural Economics
Page path slug: agriculture-economics
Researches farm investment, land tenure, production choices, and rural development.

Documented team to verify: Esteban Colla de Robertis; Akito Kamei.

### Environmental Economics
Page path slug: environmental-economics
Uses economic methods to investigate environmental conditions, climate, and policy questions.

Documented team to verify: Akito Kamei.

### Economic Demography
Page path slug: demographic-economics
Studies population, fertility, family decisions, and how demographic change relates to economic outcomes.

Documented team to verify: Akito Kamei.
